Dyson V15 Detect Review: The Smartest Cordless Vacuum of 2026?

★★★★☆ 4.5/5·May 18, 2026·10 min read

The Dyson V15 Detect doesn't just clean your floors — it counts the particles it picks up and shows you on an LCD screen. After three months of daily use in a home with two dogs, hardwood floors, and area rugs, here's whether it's worth the $749 price tag.

Laser Dust Detection: Gimmick or Game-Changer?

The headline feature is the green laser that reveals microscopic dust invisible to the naked eye. In our testing, it genuinely changed how we clean. The angled laser mounted on the Fluffy cleaner head casts shadows from dust particles, making them impossible to miss. You'll find yourself chasing dust bunnies you never knew existed — it's oddly satisfying and slightly horrifying.

Piezo Sensor & Particle Counting

The built-in piezo sensor measures and counts dust particles 15,000 times per second, categorizing them by size on the LCD display. While we initially dismissed this as marketing fluff, it actually helps you understand which areas need more attention and when to stop cleaning. The graph ramps up as you pass over high-traffic zones and drops in cleaner areas — providing real-time feedback.

Suction Power

With 240 AW of suction (up from 185 AW on the V11), the V15 is Dyson's most powerful cordless vacuum. In Auto mode, it automatically adjusts suction based on the surface and detected debris. On hardwood floors, it ramps down to preserve battery; on carpet, it kicks into high gear. We tested with Cheerios, pet hair, and fine dust — the V15 captured everything in a single pass.

Battery & Runtime

Dyson claims 60 minutes of runtime, but that's with the non-motorized tool on Eco mode. Real-world runtime: 35-40 minutes on Auto with the Fluffy head, dropping to 15-20 minutes on Boost. The removable, swappable battery is a welcome upgrade — you can buy a spare ($149) for extended cleaning sessions.

Filtration & Maintenance

The whole-machine HEPA filtration captures 99.99% of particles down to 0.3 microns. For allergy sufferers, this is a significant benefit. The 0.76L dust bin empties easily with a point-and-shoot mechanism. Washable filters need cleaning once a month — straightforward maintenance.

✅ Pros

  • Laser dust detection reveals hidden dirt
  • Excellent suction with auto-adjustment
  • Real-time particle counting on LCD
  • Removable, swappable battery
  • HEPA filtration great for allergies
  • Converts to handheld for above-floor cleaning

❌ Cons

  • $749 is expensive
  • Boost mode drains battery in 15 min
  • Small 0.76L dust bin requires frequent emptying
  • No trigger lock — must hold continuously
  • Spare battery costs $149 extra

Our Verdict

The Dyson V15 Detect is the best cordless vacuum we've tested — if you can justify the price. The laser detection and particle counting genuinely improve the cleaning experience rather than being gimmicks. For pet owners and allergy sufferers, the HEPA filtration alone may be worth the premium.

Who should buy: Pet owners, allergy sufferers, anyone who wants the most advanced cordless vacuum available.

Who should skip: Budget-conscious shoppers — excellent alternatives exist at half the price from Shark and Tineco.

FAQ

Is the V15 worth upgrading from V11?

If you want the laser, particle counter, and swappable battery — yes. If you're happy with your V11's cleaning performance, the upgrade is nice but not essential.

How often do you need to empty the bin?

In a home with pets, expect to empty it 2-3 times per full clean. The 0.76L bin is on the smaller side.
