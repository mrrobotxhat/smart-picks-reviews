iRobot Roomba j9+ Review: The Robot Vacuum That Finally Avoids Dog Poop

★★★★☆ 4.4/5·May 5, 2026·10 min read

The Roomba j9+ is iRobot's answer to the question every pet owner has asked: "What if my robot vacuum runs over dog poop?" With PrecisionVision navigation and the P.O.O.P. (Pet Owner Official Promise) guarantee, the j9+ promises to identify and avoid pet waste, cords, socks, and shoes. After two months with two Labrador retrievers who shed constantly, here's the real-world verdict.

Obstacle Avoidance: Does It Work?

In over 60 cleaning cycles, the j9+ successfully avoided: dog toys (100%), charging cables (95%), socks (100%), shoes (100%), and yes — a simulated pet accident using a realistic prop (success!). The camera-based system takes a photo of each obstacle and labels it in the app. A few charging cable misses occurred when cables were partially under furniture, but overall performance is dramatically better than previous Roomba models.

Cleaning Performance

The j9+ features 100% stronger suction than the Roomba i7, dual rubber brushes that resist hair tangling, and a Dirt Detect sensor that identifies high-traffic zones for extra cleaning. On hardwood floors, it captured 98% of visible debris in a single pass. On medium-pile carpet, it needed two passes for deeply embedded pet hair but still performed admirably.

Clean Base Auto-Empty

Included with the j9+, the Clean Base automatically empties the robot's bin into a disposable bag that holds 60 days of debris. This is the killer feature — you genuinely set it and forget it for two months. Replacement bags cost about $5 each.

✅ Pros

  • Excellent obstacle avoidance (pet waste, cords, socks)
  • Powerful suction with auto-boost on carpet
  • Clean Base holds 60 days of debris
  • Dual rubber brushes resist pet hair tangling
  • Detailed cleaning maps with room labeling
  • P.O.O.P. guarantee provides peace of mind

❌ Cons

  • $799 is expensive
  • Camera navigation needs light to work
  • No mopping (get the Combo j9+ for that)
  • Replacement bags add ongoing cost
  • Louder than some competitors on max suction

Our Verdict

The Roomba j9+ is the best robot vacuum for pet owners. The obstacle avoidance works reliably, the auto-empty base removes the chore of daily emptying, and the cleaning performance matches the price. If you have pets and want a truly hands-off cleaning experience, this is the one to buy.

Buy if: You have pets and want worry-free automated cleaning. Skip if: Budget is tight (consider Roomba i3+ at half the price) or you need mopping (look at Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra).
