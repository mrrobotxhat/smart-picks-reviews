Apple AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C) Review: 6 Months Later

★★★★☆ 4.6/5·May 10, 2026·9 min read

Six months ago, Apple refreshed the AirPods Pro 2 with USB-C, lossless audio for Vision Pro, and improved dust resistance. But the real star of the show has been the software updates: Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, and Personalized Volume. After half a year of daily use across commutes, workouts, and calls, here's our definitive review.

Sound Quality

The AirPods Pro 2's custom driver and amplifier deliver a balanced, detailed sound profile that's improved over the original. Bass is punchy without being boomy, mids are clear and natural, and treble extends nicely without harshness. The Adaptive EQ adjusts to your ear shape in real-time — a subtle but effective feature that ensures consistent sound quality.

Compared to the Sony WF-1000XM5, the AirPods have a slightly warmer, more forgiving presentation. The Sony offers more detail and a wider soundstage, but the AirPods are more fatigue-free for long listening sessions.

Active Noise Cancellation

Apple claims 2x better ANC than the original AirPods Pro. In practice, it's about a 40-50% improvement — significant but not transformative. Low-frequency rumble (airplanes, trains, AC units) is nearly eliminated. Mid-frequency noise (office chatter, coffee shop ambience) is reduced but not silenced. The Sony XM5 and Bose QC Ultra still edge out the AirPods in pure ANC performance, but the gap has narrowed considerably.

Adaptive Audio & Smart Features

Introduced in iOS 17 and refined in iOS 18, Adaptive Audio dynamically blends Transparency and ANC based on your environment. Walking through a city, it lets traffic sounds through while dampening construction noise. Conversation Awareness automatically lowers volume and enhances voices when you start speaking — it feels like magic the first time.

Personalized Volume uses machine learning to learn your preferred listening levels and adjusts automatically. After a few weeks, it's remarkably accurate.

Fit & Comfort

The new XS ear tip size and improved vent system make the AirPods Pro 2 comfortable for longer sessions than the originals. We've worn them for 4+ hours on flights without fatigue. The IP54 rating (up from IPX4) provides dust resistance alongside water resistance — a welcome upgrade for gym-goers.

Call Quality

Call quality is a standout feature. The combination of beamforming microphones and the H2 chip's computational audio makes your voice clear even in windy conditions. In our testing, call recipients consistently rated the AirPods Pro 2 as the best-sounding earbuds for calls — better than the Sony XM5, Bose QC Ultra, and even some dedicated headsets.

Battery Life

6 hours on a single charge (ANC on), with 30 hours total via the case. Fast charging gives 1 hour of listening from a 5-minute charge. The case now supports Apple Watch charger, MagSafe, and USB-C — finally, one cable for everything.

✅ Pros

  • Excellent balanced sound with Adaptive EQ
  • Class-leading Adaptive Audio and smart features
  • Best-in-class call quality
  • Comfortable for long sessions
  • USB-C + MagSafe + Apple Watch charging
  • Seamless Apple ecosystem integration

❌ Cons

  • Sony XM5 has slightly better ANC and soundstage
  • No customizable EQ (only presets)
  • Android features are limited
  • White-only color option
  • No hi-res codec support (AAC only)

Our Verdict

The AirPods Pro 2 remain the best wireless earbuds for iPhone users. While competitors edge them out in raw sound quality and ANC, no other earbuds offer the same combination of smart features, seamless integration, and excellent call quality. If you're in the Apple ecosystem, these are a no-brainer.

Who should buy: iPhone users who want the best all-around wireless earbuds.

Who should consider alternatives: Android users (look at Sony XM5), audiophiles (consider Sennheiser Momentum TW4), or those who prioritize maximum ANC (Bose QC Ultra).
