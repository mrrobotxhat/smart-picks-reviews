Smart Picks uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve our content and user experience.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Smart Picks is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
